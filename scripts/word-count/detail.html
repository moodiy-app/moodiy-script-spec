What it does

Counts the words in the body of the entry and tells you how many files are attached.

Result

  • Message: for example 42 words, 2 attachment(s): a.png, b.png.
  • Output: the number of words, as text, for the next step.
  • An entry without a body is skipped.

A JavaScript script. It runs inside Moodiy and cannot reach the network, your files or other programs.
